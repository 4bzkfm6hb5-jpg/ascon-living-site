A house with river view: your private rooftop terrace in Ho Chi Minh City

Approx. 56–60 m² at the top of the house, only for you: BBQ and smoker, an outdoor sofa with seven cushions, padded stools, a dining table, long planters and solar lights, with the river and the city skyline in front of you.

BBQ and smoker

The black barrel grill stands at the railing, with a side shelf for plates and tongs.

A skyline barbecue

Marinate meat, seafood and vegetables in the kitchen, carry them up and grill while the sun goes down behind the skyline.

Slow smoking

Use the smoker for a longer cook: ribs or a whole chicken while the group sits on the sofa.

Vegetables and corn for the kids

Grilled corn, sweet potatoes and vegetable skewers are quick and children can help turn them.

The outdoor sofa and stools

A large outdoor sofa in dark rattan with seven cream cushions forms the lounge corner. Padded cream stools stand around a low table and can be moved where you need them.

Sundowner

Cold drinks from the 540-litre fridge, everyone on the sofa, and the city lights coming on one after another.

Morning coffee

Before the heat of the day, take your coffee up and enjoy the view in the quiet.

A birthday or anniversary

Decorate the lounge corner, put the cake on the low table and toast with the whole group above the city. The stools can be pulled up so that everyone sits in one circle.

A quiet hour with a book

In the afternoon, when the others are out, the sofa under the awning is a shaded place to read.

The dining table

A dark table with rattan seating and cream cushions stands under the covered part of the terrace.

Dinner under the sky

Eat what came off the grill straight at the table, with the skyline behind you.

Breakfast outside

Fruit from the market, bread and coffee on the rooftop instead of a hotel breakfast room.

River and District 1 skyline

From the rooftop you look over the river to the skyline of District 1. In the evening the high-rises light up in colour.

Photos with the skyline

The skyline at night makes the best background for group photos and holiday pictures.

Watching the city lights

Sit back and watch the towers change colour after dark. It is easy to come up for a short look and stay all evening.

Plants, solar lights and the awning

Long planters with tropical plants line the edge of the terrace in front of white metal railings. Solar lights and string lights under the awning switch the terrace to a warm glow in the evening. Part of the terrace is covered by an awning, which gives shade in the afternoon and shelter during a short shower.

Rainy season on the roof

When a short tropical shower passes, move to the covered part with the dining table and wait until the air is fresh again.

How the rooftop works with the rest of the house

The rooftop extends the living space of the house into the open air. The kitchen supplies the BBQ and the cold drinks, the living room and the entertainment room take over when it gets late, and the bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms are only a few floors down. Please keep the volume considerate for the neighbours in the evening, so the terrace stays a calm place for everyone.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the rooftop terrace?

Approx. 56–60 m², private, only for your group.

Can we barbecue on the rooftop?

Yes, there is a BBQ with smoker on the terrace.

What can we see from the rooftop?

The river and the District 1 skyline, especially beautiful at night.
