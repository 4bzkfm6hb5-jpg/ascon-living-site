Whole house rental Ho Chi Minh: group accommodation in Saigon

Friends' trip, family reunion or team getaway: ASCON LIVING gives your group a private house with three en-suite bedrooms, an entertainment room and a rooftop terrace for long evenings together.

One house, one group, no strangers

Travelling as a group is about time together, and that is hard when everyone is scattered across hotel rooms and corridors. At ASCON LIVING your group has the entire house: over 250 m² across three floors plus a rooftop terrace, shared with nobody but you. If you have been looking for a private house for groups in Ho Chi Minh City, this is a home with exactly that kind of space.

For friends

Everyone under one roof, from the first coffee to the last conversation on the rooftop.

For family reunions

Siblings, cousins and grandparents together in one house, with their own rooms and bathrooms.

For couples travelling together

Two or three couples on a shared trip each get a bedroom with its own bathroom, and still share the living room, the kitchen and the rooftop. It is the easiest way to travel together without giving up privacy.

Privacy for everyone

Each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom and its own air conditioning. The master bedroom is about 55 m² with a 180×200 cm bed, a 55-inch 4K TV and a balcony. Bedrooms 2 and 3 have 160×200 cm beds and smart TVs; bedroom 3 is an interior room without a window, dark and quiet for those who want to sleep in. There is also a guest WC in the living area.

No bathroom queue

Nobody fights over the shower, and nobody wakes the others when they head out early.

Karaoke nights, game tournaments and movie marathons

The entertainment room is your private stage. Start with karaoke: duets to warm up, then a proper karaoke competition with a jury on the sofa. When voices need a rest, switch to a PlayStation tournament with PlayStation Plus, or a movie marathon with Netflix.

Good timing

Loud music only until 21:30, so plan the big songs early and finish the night with a film or a game.

Rooftop BBQ with the District 1 skyline

The rooftop terrace of about 56–60 m² is where group trips become memories. Fire up the BBQ or the smoker, gather on the outdoor sofa and watch the lights of the District 1 skyline come on across the river. Celebrate a birthday, a reunion with old friends or the end of a successful project. Solar lights keep the mood warm after dark.

Shared cooking

Stock the fridge of about 540 litres on day one, then take turns: one team grills, one team clears up.

For colleagues and work trips

The house works just as well for business teams. WiFi up to 1 Gbit/s with mesh on every floor handles video calls and uploads from any room. The living room and dining table double as a meeting space, the bedrooms are quiet, and an iron is ready before the client meeting. After work, the rooftop beats any hotel bar.

Easy arrival and everyday comfort

Self check-in with a keypad means your group can arrive at any time, with a key for the outer gate. An airport transfer can be arranged with your booking, which helps when several people arrive with luggage. Fresh bedding, towels, toiletries, slippers and hot water are ready, guests may use the washing machine, and a laundry service is available for 30 EUR per load. There are outdoor cameras at the entrance. Restaurants and street food are nearby, and the free ASCON Guide App helps you find food, bars and things to do.

Frequently asked questions

Is the whole house private for our group?

Yes. You book the entire house, including the rooftop terrace and the entertainment room.

Does everyone get their own bathroom?

Each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom, and there is a guest WC in the living area, equipped like the other bathrooms.

Can we use the karaoke in the evening?

Yes. Loud music only until 21:30, so plan the singing for the early evening.
