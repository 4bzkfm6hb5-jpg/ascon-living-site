A Saigon homestay with the whole house to yourselves

At ASCON LIVING you don't book a room, you book the house. Over 250 m² of living space across three floors plus a rooftop terrace, and every square metre belongs to you for the length of your stay. No reception desk, no shared corridors, no strangers at breakfast.

For families

Three bedrooms with their own bathrooms, a real kitchen and plenty of space for the kids to play. Read more on our page for families.

For groups of friends

Karaoke, PS5 (PlayStation 5), a big living room and a rooftop BBQ: everything for long evenings together. Read more on our page for groups.

For couples

Space, privacy and a master bedroom of about 55 m² with its own balcony. A city break that feels like having your own home in Saigon.

For remote workers

WiFi up to 1 Gbit/s with mesh on every floor, quiet bedrooms and a dining table that doubles as a desk.

Inside the house, room by room

Every room has its own page with photos and details:

  • Living room (about 40 m²): a large L-shaped sofa and a 75-inch 4K TV, plus a guest WC in the living area, equipped like the other bathrooms.
  • Kitchen and dining: stove, oven, microwave, rice cooker, kettle, dishes and a fridge of about 540 litres.
  • Entertainment room: karaoke, 4K cinema system (Optoma 4K projector), PS5 (PlayStation 5) with PlayStation Plus, Netflix and movie nights.
  • Master bedroom (about 55 m²): 180×200 cm bed, 55-inch 4K TV, balcony, own bathroom.
  • Bedroom 2: 160×200 cm bed, smart TV, own bathroom.
  • Bedroom 3: an interior room without a window, dark and quiet, 160×200 cm bed, smart TV, own bathroom.
  • Bathrooms: one for every bedroom, plus a guest WC.
  • Rooftop terrace (about 56–60 m²): BBQ, smoker, outdoor sofa, plants, solar lights, river and skyline view.

Evenings you will remember

The best moments of a trip often happen after dark. Start with a BBQ on the rooftop while the lights of District 1 come on across the river. Move downstairs to the entertainment room for duets and a friendly karaoke competition. Later, switch to a PS5 (PlayStation 5) tournament or a film with Netflix. Loud music only until 21:30, which suits the rhythm of the evening: sing early, then wind down with a game or a movie.

For families

For groups

A rooftop dinner followed by a karaoke round is the classic ASCON LIVING evening.

Easy arrival, everyday comfort

Arriving should be the easy part. Check in yourself with the keypad lock, whenever you land, and use the key for the outer gate. An airport transfer can be arranged with your booking. Inside, everything is ready: fresh bedding, towels, toiletries, slippers, hot water, an iron and a safe. A laundry service is available on request.

There are outdoor cameras at the entrance.

Frequently asked questions

Do we really get the whole house?

Yes. You book the entire private house, including the rooftop terrace, for your group only.

Does every bedroom have its own bathroom?

Yes, all three bedrooms have their own bathroom and air conditioning, and there is a guest WC in the living area, equipped like the other bathrooms.

How do we check in?

Self check-in with a keypad lock; you also receive a key for the outer gate. Instructions follow after booking.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes. An airport transfer from Tân Sơn Nhất can be arranged on request together with your booking.

Is the house for a small group?

Yes. The whole house is for one small group, such as a family or friends travelling together. You do not share it with other guests.
