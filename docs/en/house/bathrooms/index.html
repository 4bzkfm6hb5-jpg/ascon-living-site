Three en-suite bathrooms and a guest WC: no queue in your house in Saigon

Each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom, and there is a guest WC in the living area, equipped the same way as the other bathrooms. Matte black showers, round black-framed mirrors, wall-mounted hair dryers, dispensers and white towels on the rails: here is what you find and how it makes mornings easier.

One bathroom per bedroom

In a house with several couples or a family with teenagers, the bathroom is often the bottleneck. Here each bedroom has its own.

Mornings without a queue

Everyone gets ready at the same time in their own bathroom, and the group meets in the kitchen for breakfast.

Privacy for every guest

Grandparents, a second couple or friends do not need to share a bathroom with anyone outside their room.

At night

Nobody needs to cross the hallway in the dark. The bathroom is right next to the bed.

The shower

A large square matte black shower head hangs from the ceiling bar, with a hand shower on the same rail. Two black dispensers are mounted on the wall, with a black shelf for your own bottles.

Washing off the city

After a hot day in Saigon, stand under the shower and wash off the heat and the dust of the streets.

Quick rinse with the hand shower

Rinse sandy feet after the market or wash a child's hair without getting everyone wet.

Mirror, shelf and vanity

A round black-framed mirror hangs above the white vanity, with a glass shelf below and a black cup holding wrapped toothbrush sets.

Getting ready

Shave, put on make-up or style your hair in front of the mirror, with space on the shelf for your things.

Brushing teeth after a long flight

Wrapped toothbrush sets stand in the cup, useful if your toiletry bag is still at the bottom of the suitcase after a late arrival.

Hair dryer and towels

A hair dryer is mounted on the wall, so it is always in the same place and does not need to be searched for. White towels hang on wall rails and hooks.

After the rooftop

Dry your hair quickly before dinner, without borrowing a dryer from another room.

For families

Towels hang on rails and hooks within reach, so children can find theirs after the shower without calling for help.

Design

Light cream marble-look tiles cover the walls. Matte black fittings, the black-framed mirror and the black cup follow the house design of light base colours with black accents, calm and hotel-like. The light tiles and black fittings match the style of the bedrooms, so the bathroom feels like part of the room, not an add-on.

The guest WC

The guest WC lies in the living area and is equipped the same way as the other bathrooms. Visitors and children use it during the day without going to a bedroom. Bedroom bathrooms stay private for the people who sleep there.

How the bathrooms work with the rest of the house

Three bedrooms, three en-suite bathrooms, one guest WC: everyone in the group has their own space, while the living room, the kitchen, the entertainment room and the rooftop stay the shared areas. Families, friends travelling together and colleagues on a business trip can share a whole house without sharing a bathroom.

Frequently asked questions

How many bathrooms does the house have?

Each of the three bedrooms has its own en-suite bathroom, and there is a guest WC in the living area, equipped the same way as the other bathrooms.

What is in the bathrooms?

A matte black shower with hand shower, wall dispensers, a round mirror, a white vanity, a wall-mounted hair dryer and white towels.

Do I have to share a bathroom with other guests?

No. You book the whole house, and each bedroom has its own bathroom.
