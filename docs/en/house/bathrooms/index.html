Three private bathrooms and a guest WC: no queue in your Saigon homestay

Each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom, and there is a guest WC in the living area. Hot water, fresh towels and toiletries are ready, so mornings stay relaxed for everyone.

What's in this room

  • Three en-suite bathrooms, one per bedroom
  • Guest WC in the living area
  • Rain shower with hand shower
  • Hot water
  • Fresh towels
  • Toiletries and slippers
  • Hair dryer

A closer look at the bathrooms

The bathrooms share one look: large cream marble-look tiles on the walls, matte-black fittings and white towels. Each shower has a square black rain shower head, a hand shower on a black rail, two wall-mounted black dispensers for shower products, a black shelf and a squeegee.

A round mirror with a thin black frame hangs above the basin, with a glass shelf underneath holding a black cup of individually packed toothbrush kits. Fresh white towels hang on the rails, and a hair dryer is mounted on the wall next to the white vanity cabinet. Hot water is always available and slippers are ready.

One bathroom for every bedroom

Anyone who has shared a holiday apartment with one bathroom knows the morning routine: knocking on the door, checking the time, someone always last in line. At ASCON LIVING that does not happen. Each of the three bedrooms has its own private bathroom, and the living area has an extra guest WC.

For families

Parents, kids and grandparents each have their own bathroom. Bath time, teeth brushing and getting dressed all happen at the same time, without stress.

For groups of friends

Every couple or pair has their own bathroom, which keeps a shared trip comfortable, even for people who do not usually live together.

A shower after a day in the city

Ho Chi Minh City is warm and humid, and after a day of walking, markets and sightseeing, a shower is the best part of coming home. Hot water is always available, so you can rinse off the heat before heading out again for dinner or going up to the rooftop.

For couples

A cool shower, fresh clothes and an evening on the rooftop: the simple routine of a perfect holiday evening.

For remote workers

A quick shower between the morning and afternoon calls is an easy way to feel fresh again.

Everything ready when you arrive

You do not need to pack a full toiletry bag. Fresh towels and toiletries are provided, slippers are ready, and hot water is always available. A laundry service is available on request, which helps on longer stays or when you are travelling light.

For families

Fewer things to pack means more room in the suitcase for the things the kids cannot live without.

For business travellers

Arrive with hand luggage only, and the basics are already there.

The guest WC in the living area

The living area has its own guest WC. During the day, guests and children do not need to walk up to a bedroom bathroom, and visitors you invite for a drink can use it without going through anyone's bedroom.

For families

Small children who need the toilet in a hurry during a game or a film do not have to climb the stairs. Parents with toddlers will appreciate it more than almost anything else in the house.

For groups

When everyone is downstairs for karaoke, a film or dinner, the guest WC keeps people from wandering through the bedrooms.

Privacy and safety

Because each bathroom belongs to one bedroom, everyone has their own space for their things. Cameras are installed outdoors only, never inside the house, so your privacy is respected.

For couples

Your own bathroom means your own pace: a long shower, time to get ready for a dinner out, and no one waiting outside the door.

For groups

Everyone can leave their things where they belong, so nothing gets mixed up during the stay.

A morning with three bathrooms

7:00, the early riser showers without waking anyone. 7:30, parents get the kids ready in their own bathroom while the grandparents use theirs. 8:00, a friend who arrived late last night is still asleep in bedroom 3, and nobody needs to knock on that door. By 8:30 everyone is in the kitchen at the same time, ready for breakfast, and nobody has spent the morning waiting in a corridor. That is the difference between sharing a house and sharing a bathroom.
