What you can do in the karaoke room

Sing together and perform duets

Take turns or sing together. Duets work with the microphones provided: pick a song, stand up in front of the sofa and let the others cheer.

Private karaoke nights

For families, friends and couples who want an evening of music at home instead of a karaoke bar. The room is yours, with no other guests waiting for the next slot.

Which songs and languages are available depends on the app or source you use.

Family karaoke

Children pick the songs, parents sing along, grandparents judge from the sofa. Everyone gets a turn with the microphone, and nobody needs to be a good singer.

Small contests

Let favourite songs compete, score each other's duets or hold a karaoke contest with a winner at the end of the night.

Record your performances

Film your performance with your smartphone and keep the funniest moments for the group chat.

PS5 (PlayStation 5) with PlayStation Plus

The PS5 (PlayStation 5) hangs on the wall next to the TV, with two controllers on their mount below.

Game night with friends

Play against each other or as a team, with the whole group on the sofa watching and commenting.

A tournament between karaoke rounds

Alternate: one round of singing, one round of gaming. The loser picks the next song.

A rainy afternoon

When a tropical downpour hits the city, the PS5 (PlayStation 5) and the air-conditioned room turn a lost afternoon into a game session.

Quiet gaming for teenagers

While the adults sit on the rooftop, teenagers can play in a room of their own.

The 4K cinema system (Optoma 4K projector) for movies and big-screen nights

A 4K cinema system (Optoma 4K projector) is mounted on the ceiling, and a projection screen on a tripod stands in front of the wall.

Movie night

Switch off the light, keep the colour LEDs low and watch a film on the big screen. Snacks go on the stone-look coffee tables.

Big-screen nights

Concert films, a series marathon or a match: the projector screen makes the room feel like a small private cinema.

Design and atmosphere

Grey walls, a grey sectional sofa with blue-grey cushions, dark cube stools and a blue-grey carpet keep the room calm. Framed anime and comic posters hang on the walls, lit by warm wall lights. Colour LED light lines run across walls and ceiling and give the room a party feel at night.

How the devices combine

You switch between karaoke, gaming and film without leaving the room. The living room adds a second screen, the 75-inch 4K TV, and each bedroom has its own TV, so one group can sing while another watches a series elsewhere in the house. Karaoke ends when the house rules ask for quiet, and the evening continues on the rooftop or in the living room.
