Riverside in Ho Chi Minh City: terraces and the river in front of your house

ASCON LIVING stands at the river. Terraces with white metal railings and awnings look across the water to the city skyline, and the private rooftop sits above it all.

What's here

The river in front of the house

From the terraces you look across the water to the opposite bank and further to the high-rises of District 1.

The skyline after dark

In the evening the towers of District 1 light up in colour behind the water. The rooftop is the best place for it.

Photos with water and city

Water, green banks and the skyline make a background for holiday photos that you do not get from a hotel room in the city centre.

Terraces with white railings

Tiled terraces with white metal railings run along the front of the house. Awnings shade parts of them.

Fresh air between two outings

Step outside for a moment, look at the water and feel the breeze before the next tour.

A drink in the evening

Take a cold drink from the fridge and stand at the railing while the city lights come on.

The rooftop

At the top of the house the private rooftop of approx. 56–60 m² has a BBQ and smoker, an outdoor sofa with seven cushions, padded stools, a dining table, long planters and solar lights. It has the widest view over the river and the District 1 skyline. All details are on the rooftop page.

Outdoor cameras

There are outdoor cameras at the entrance.

Frequently asked questions

Is the house really at the river?

Yes. From the terraces and the rooftop you look across the river to the District 1 skyline.

Where is the best view of the skyline?

On the rooftop, especially after dark when the towers of District 1 light up.
