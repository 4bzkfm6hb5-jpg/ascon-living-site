The living room: 40 m², L-sofa and 75-inch 4K TV in your house in Ho Chi Minh City

Approx. 40 m², a large L-shaped sofa, a 75-inch 4K TV with Netflix, a guest WC and air conditioning, in grey, white and greige with black accents. Here is what is in the room and what you can do with it.

The L-shaped sofa: one seat for everyone

The sofa runs along two walls and faces the TV, so nobody watches from the side. Its white leather-look surface is easy to wipe after snacks, and the olive-green and beige cushions add a little colour to the grey and white room.

Movie night with the whole group

Everyone gets a place on one sofa. Dim the warm cove lights, put drinks and snacks on the glass coffee table and start the film.

Feet up after a day in the city

Saigon is hot and humid. Come back, switch on the air conditioning and stretch out along the long side of the L. The short side stays free for whoever arrives next.

Planning tomorrow

Spread the map and phones out on the coffee table, sit around it on the sofa and decide together where to eat and what to see.

The 75-inch 4K TV with Netflix

The TV hangs on a wall of black fluted panels, framed by two tall tropical plants, above a long low sideboard. At 75 inches in 4K it is big enough to watch together from anywhere on the sofa.

Series and films on Netflix

Continue the series you started at home or choose a film together. The mesh WiFi with up to 1 Gbit/s keeps streaming smooth even when others are online at the same time.

Match day

Football, a race or a final: the whole group watches on one big screen instead of on phones.

Cartoon morning for the kids

Children watch their show on the big screen while breakfast is made in the kitchen next door.

Glass doors, light and design

Floor-to-ceiling glass doors with dark grey curtains open towards the green outside. Draw the curtains for a film, open them in the morning for daylight.

A calm, hotel-like look

Cream walls, grey marble-look floor tiles with black skirting, black wall panels and white furniture follow the house colour scheme of grey, white and greige with matte black accents. A framed colourful art print, a wall clock and small decor pieces on the sideboard keep it personal without clutter.

Plants instead of ornaments

Large tropical plants in white pots stand on both sides of the TV and beside the sofa. They bring green into the grey and white room.

Guest WC and air conditioning

Guest WC in the living area

The guest WC is right in the living area and is equipped the same way as the other bathrooms. Children and visitors do not need to go up to a bedroom bathroom during the day.

Air conditioning

A wall unit cools the living room, so the sofa stays a comfortable place even on the hottest afternoons.

Working from the sofa

Mesh WiFi up to 1 Gbit/s

The mesh WiFi with extra nodes covers all three floors. Answer emails on the sofa in the morning or join a video call while the others are out. For longer work sessions, the desk in the anteroom offers a proper work area.

How the living room works with the rest of the house

The living room connects to the kitchen and the long dining table for shared meals. For karaoke, the PlayStation and the 4K projector, go to the entertainment room. For sunset and the District 1 skyline, go up to the rooftop. Bedrooms with en-suite bathrooms keep the nights private, so the living room can stay the shared space during the day.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the living room and what is in it?

Approx. 40 m², with a large L-shaped sofa, a 75-inch 4K TV with Netflix, a glass coffee table, air conditioning and a guest WC.

Is there a toilet on the living room floor?

Yes, there is a guest WC in the living area, equipped the same way as the other bathrooms, in addition to the en-suite bathroom of each bedroom.

Can I work from the living room?

Yes. The house has mesh WiFi with up to 1 Gbit/s on all floors, and there is also a desk in the anteroom.
