Master bedroom with balcony: 55 m² and a 180×200 cm bed in Ho Chi Minh City

Approx. 55 m², a 180×200 cm bed, a 55-inch 4K TV, your own balcony, an en-suite bathroom and your own air conditioning. Here is every item in the largest bedroom of the house and how you can use it.

The 180×200 cm bed

The bed stands in the middle of the room with a cream upholstered headboard, white linen, a grey runner across the foot and grey cushions. Bedside tables with black lamps stand on both sides, so each sleeper has a light and a place for a phone.

Sleeping in after a late night

After karaoke or a rooftop evening, close the curtains, switch on the air conditioning and sleep as long as you want.

Room for a small child

Parents with a toddler have space in a 180 cm wide bed for a morning cuddle.

The 55-inch 4K TV

The TV hangs on the wall opposite the bed.

A film in bed

Watch a film from bed while the others are still in the living room.

Morning news with coffee

Check the weather and the news before you plan the day.

Streaming without disturbing the others

The mesh WiFi with up to 1 Gbit/s reaches every floor, so streaming in the bedroom runs smoothly while the rest of the group uses the big screens elsewhere in the house.

The balcony

The master bedroom has its own balcony.

First coffee outside

Step out with a cup before the rest of the house wakes up and feel the morning air of the city.

A quiet phone call

Call home from the balcony without waking anyone inside.

The dressing table

A grey and black dressing table with a round illuminated mirror stands in its own corner, with a white armchair in front of it. A wireless charging pad is built into the table top.

Getting ready for the evening

Make-up and hair under a lit mirror, without queueing for the bathroom.

Charge your phone without a cable

Put a compatible phone on the pad while you get ready.

Wardrobe, iron and towels

A wooden wardrobe holds spare towels, an iron and an ironing board.

Unpacking for a longer stay

Hang your clothes and fill the shelves instead of living out of a suitcase.

A pressed shirt for a business meeting

Iron your shirt in the room before you leave.

En-suite bathroom and air conditioning

The en-suite bathroom lies behind a black fluted panel wall, so you go from bed to shower without crossing the hallway. The room has its own air conditioning, and a standing fan adds a breeze if you prefer it.

Design and light

Large windows with grey-brown curtains let in daylight. Cream walls, grey marble-look floor tiles with black skirting, a green rug, a plant and green framed art follow the house style of grey, white and greige with black accents. The result looks calm and hotel-like, with the space of a private house. With about 55 m² there is room to open two suitcases on the floor and still walk freely between bed, wardrobe and dressing table.

How the master bedroom works with the rest of the house

The master bedroom is the retreat for a couple or for parents. Two more bedrooms, each with an en-suite bathroom, give the rest of the group privacy. The living room, the entertainment room and the rooftop are the shared spaces.
