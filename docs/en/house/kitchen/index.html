Kitchen and dining in your house in Ho Chi Minh City: cook, eat and sit together

Stove, oven, microwave, rice cooker, kettle, air fryer, toaster, a side-by-side fridge of approx. 540 litres, dishes for everyone and a long dining table. Here is every item and what you can cook with it.

Cooking: stove, oven and the small helpers

The L-shaped counter gives two people room to cook at once: one at the glass cooktop, one at the sink. Above the worktop the black-and-white patterned tiles run along both walls, under dark grey cabinets.

A market dinner on the cooktop

Bring herbs, vegetables and seafood from the local market and cook them the way you like. Pots and pans are in the cabinets.

Baking and roasting in the oven

Roast vegetables, warm bread or bake something for the kids' afternoon.

Quick breakfast

The kettle boils water for tea or instant coffee, the toaster browns the bread, and the air fryer makes crispy eggs or snacks without a pan.

Rice cooker and microwave

Rice for the whole table

The rice cooker makes rice to go with Vietnamese dishes, whether cooked at home or brought back from the street kitchen.

Leftovers and take-away

The microwave stands on a black shelf rack above the air fryer. Warm up yesterday's banh mi fillings or the soup that was too much for dinner.

The 540-litre fridge

The black side-by-side fridge of approx. 540 litres has two tall doors and space for a group's shopping.

Shopping for several days

One big trip to the supermarket fills the fridge for several days, so nobody has to go out for every meal.

Cold drinks for the rooftop

Water, juice and beer stay cold until you carry them up to the rooftop for sunset.

BBQ preparation

Marinated meat and vegetables wait in the fridge until the grill on the rooftop is hot.

Dishes and utensils

Cabinets hold plates, bowls and glasses for a full table, plus pots and pans. A knife block, a utensil rack and chopsticks stand ready on the counter.

Family breakfast

Cereal bowls for the kids, plates for the adults, glasses for juice: everything is in one place.

Cooking for a group

When several friends cook together, one cuts vegetables at the counter, one stirs at the cooktop and one sets the long table. There are enough plates and glasses for everyone to eat at the same time.

The dining table

A long marble-look table with cream upholstered chairs stands in front of floor-to-ceiling glass doors with grey curtains. A ceiling fan turns overhead, white fluted panels line the lower walls, and a black staircase railing frames the room on one side.

Long dinners together

The whole group eats at one table. Share dishes in the middle, the way Vietnamese meals are eaten.

A table for planning and games

Between meals the table is a place for cards, homework, laptops or planning tomorrow's tour.

Laundry corner

A front-loading washing machine stands in the corner of the kitchen area, with laundry bags beside it. Guests are welcome to use it. Practical on longer stays and for families with children.

Prefer not to wash yourself? A laundry service is available for 30 EUR per load.

How the kitchen works with the rest of the house

The kitchen is next to the living room, so the cook still hears the TV and the conversation. Drinks and food go up to the rooftop for BBQ evenings. After dinner the group moves on to the entertainment room for karaoke or the PS5 (PlayStation 5).

Frequently asked questions

What appliances does the kitchen have?

A stove with glass cooktop, oven, microwave, rice cooker, kettle, air fryer, toaster, dishwasher, coffee machine, washing machine and a side-by-side fridge of approx. 540 litres.

Can we wash clothes?

Yes. Guests are welcome to use the front-loading washing machine in the kitchen area, and a laundry service is available for 30 EUR per load.

Are dishes and cookware provided?

Yes. Plates, bowls, glasses, pots and pans are in the cabinets, plus a knife block, utensils and chopsticks.

Is there a dining table for everyone?

Yes, a long marble-look table with upholstered chairs stands next to the kitchen.
