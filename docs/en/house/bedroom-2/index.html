Bedroom 2: comfort with its own bathroom in your Ho Chi Minh house rental

A 160×200 cm bed, a smart TV, a private bathroom and its own air conditioning. Bedroom 2 gives a second couple, two siblings or the grandparents a room of their own.

What's in this room

  • Bed 160 × 200 cm with upholstered headboard
  • Smart TV
  • En-suite bathroom
  • Own air conditioning and a standing fan
  • Window with dark grey curtains
  • Wardrobe with open shelves and fresh towels

A closer look at bedroom 2

Bedroom 2 is a calm room in warm greige. The 160 × 200 cm bed has an upholstered headboard, white bedding with a grey runner and dark grey cushions, and stands on a green-and-white rug. A window with dark grey curtains brings in daylight. The smart TV hangs on the wall beside the bathroom door, which is covered in a vivid print of autumn leaves in orange, red and green.

Two framed paintings of green forest and waterside landscapes hang above the bed and on the side wall. A light grey wardrobe with open shelves holds fresh towels. A standing fan, a potted plant and the room's own air conditioner complete the setup.

A room of your own, inside a shared holiday

Travelling together is wonderful, but everyone needs a door they can close. Bedroom 2 is a comfortable, well-sized room with a 160×200 cm bed, its own air conditioning and its own bathroom. That combination is what makes a shared house work: you spend the day together and still have a private space to return to.

The art wall gives the room a warm, personal feel. It looks like someone's home, not a hotel corridor.

For families

Grandparents get a calm room close to the grandchildren but with their own bathroom. Or two siblings share it as "their" room for the holiday.

For groups of friends

A second couple gets the same privacy as the first: their own room, their own shower, their own schedule.

The en-suite bathroom makes the difference

Each of the three bedrooms has its own bathroom, and bedroom 2 is no exception. It sounds like a detail, but on a shared trip it changes the whole morning. Nobody knocks on the door, nobody waits for the shower, and everyone gets ready at their own pace. Hot water, fresh towels and toiletries are ready.

For remote workers

An early call, a shower, a coffee, all without disturbing anyone else in the house.

For families

Bath time for small children happens in your own bathroom, at your own time.

Winding down with a smart TV

A smart TV with Netflix makes it easy to end the day in your own room: a cartoon for the kids before bed, a few episodes of a series for the grown-ups, or the news from home.

For couples

After a day of sightseeing and a big dinner, sometimes the best plan is a film in your own room.

For teenagers

A smart TV in their own room is often the key to a happy teenager on a family trip.

Sleep well, wake up rested

Your own air conditioning lets you set the temperature exactly the way you like it, which matters in the tropical climate of Ho Chi Minh City. Fresh bedding and slippers are ready when you arrive. Loud music is only allowed until 21:30, so the evenings are calm and everyone gets a good night's sleep before the next day in the city.

For groups

If part of the group goes out late, the rest can sleep undisturbed, because each bedroom is its own private space.

Everything you need for the stay

The house provides an iron for the evening you want to look smart, a safe for valuables, hot water and toiletries. Laundry service is available on request for longer stays, and WiFi of up to 1 Gbit/s with mesh on every floor keeps everyone connected.

A day in bedroom 2

Imagine a family reunion. The grandparents take bedroom 2. In the morning they are up first, shower in their own bathroom without waking anyone, and enjoy a quiet coffee in the kitchen before the grandchildren come downstairs. During the day everyone explores the city together. In the afternoon the grandparents retreat to their room for a rest, set the air conditioning to a comfortable temperature and watch something on the smart TV, while the kids are busy in the entertainment room. In the evening the whole family meets on the rooftop for dinner, and later everyone has a quiet room to go back to. That balance of time together and time apart is what makes a whole house so relaxing.
