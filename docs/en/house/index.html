The house: an entire villa in Ho Chi Minh City

The entire house, is for your group only.

Living Room

Start the day or wind down in a bright living room with a large L-shaped sofa, TV and big windows facing the greenery – enough seating for the whole group.

Kitchen & Dining

Cook together in a fully equipped kitchen with fridge, dishwasher, oven, stove, microwave, coffee machine, kettle and rice cooker, then sit down at one table for shared meals.

Bedroom 1

A calm retreat with a double bed, air conditioning and its own vanity corner.

Bedroom 2

Double bed, air conditioning and a TV – comfortable for a quiet evening in your own room.

Bedroom 3

Double bed, air conditioning and a TV, with fresh towels and bed linen provided.

Bathrooms

Bathrooms with rain showers, towels and hair dryers mean no queueing in the morning, even with a full house. Handrails are installed.

Entertainment Room

A separate room for evenings together – a villa with karaoke: sing, watch a film on the projector screen or play on the PlayStation.

Rooftop & River View

Terrace with a lounge, outdoor dining area and BBQ grill, plus views across the river and the skyline – a house with river view from the top floor and three balconies.

Entrance & Hallway

Self check-in via keypad leads you into the entrance; stairs with handrails connect the floors.

Amenities

  • Air conditioning and fans
  • Wi-Fi
  • Washing machine and iron
  • Safe
  • Smoke detector, fire extinguisher, first-aid kit
  • Self check-in via keypad (the outer gate uses a key)
  • BBQ on the rooftop
