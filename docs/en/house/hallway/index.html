Anteroom and work area: a desk, two bedroom doors and the stairs of your house

Between the bedrooms lies an anteroom with a work desk by the window, an office chair, crane prints and plants. Add the entrance with shoe rack, a dressing corner by the glass doors and dark granite stairs with black railings, and you know every space of the house.

The work area

The desk stands by a window with grey curtains, on a beige and green patterned rug. A black mesh office chair and a desk lamp make it a real workplace, not a dining table borrowed for the laptop. Three framed prints with white cranes on grey hang on the wall, and plants on black stands keep the corner green.

Video calls from Saigon

The mesh WiFi with up to 1 Gbit/s covers all three floors. Join the morning meeting with the team at home while the rest of the house sleeps.

A longer stay with remote work

Digital nomads and guests who work during their holiday have a fixed desk with daylight, separate from the bedrooms and the living room.

Homework and travel planning

Children write their holiday diary at the desk, adults book tickets and tours for the next day.

The anteroom and the two bedroom doors

Two black panel doors stand side by side and lead into bedroom 2 and bedroom 3. Grey marble-look floor tiles with black skirting and cream walls follow the house colours.

Short ways at night

From the bedroom door to the stairs it is only a few steps across the anteroom.

Quiet in the evening

Whoever goes to bed early closes the black door; the others stay in the living room or on the rooftop.

The dressing corner

A grey dressing table with a round mirror and a white chair stands by the glass doors with grey curtains.

Getting ready in daylight

Make-up and hair by the glass doors, with daylight on your face, while others use the bathrooms.

Entrance and shoe rack

Black fluted wall panels frame the entrance. A built-in cabinet with hooks holds bags and jackets, and a black shoe rack with house slippers stands next to a bench and a door mat.

Arriving with luggage

Put the suitcases down, take off your shoes on the bench, change into house slippers and keep the street dust outside.

The stairs

Dark granite steps with black metal railings connect the floors. Wall lights and black decorative 3D panels in the stairwell continue the black accents of the house.

Day and night

Wall lights light the stairs in the evening, so nobody climbs in the dark.

How the anteroom works with the rest of the house

The anteroom links bedrooms, stairs and work area. It keeps the bedrooms private and gives remote workers their own desk away from the TV and the kitchen. Business travellers can prepare a presentation at the desk in the morning and still join the group for the rooftop BBQ in the evening.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a desk for working?

Yes, a desk with office chair and desk lamp by a window in the anteroom, with mesh WiFi up to 1 Gbit/s.

How do the floors connect?

By dark granite stairs with black metal railings and wall lights.

Is there a place for shoes and luggage at the entrance?

Yes, a shoe rack with house slippers, a bench and a built-in cabinet with hooks.
