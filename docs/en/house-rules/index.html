House rules for your stay in Ho Chi Minh City

A few simple rules so that every group enjoys the house, and the neighbours enjoy having you.

Why we have house rules

ASCON LIVING is a private house in a residential area of Tân Hưng (District 7). You have the whole house to yourselves, with karaoke, a PlayStation, a 4K projector and a rooftop with a BBQ. A few simple rules help everyone get the most out of it: you can enjoy your evenings, the neighbours sleep well, and the house stays in good shape for the next guests.

For families

Quiet evenings mean children sleep well after a long day in the city.

For groups

The rules are easy to plan around: celebrate early, wind down later.

Loud music only until 21:30

Loud music only until 21:30. This is the one rule that shapes the evening, and it fits naturally: sing the big karaoke songs and play music on the rooftop in the early evening, then switch to a film, a PlayStation game or a quiet conversation under the solar lights.

For couples and remote workers

A calm house in the evening means a good night's sleep and a fresh start for the next day.

Arrival and departure

  • Self check-in with the keypad lock; please use the key for the outer gate and close the gate behind you.
  • Check-in between 14:00 and 23:00, check-out by 12:00.
  • Only registered guests may stay in the house; guest registration at check-in is required by Vietnamese law.

In the house

  • No smoking indoors; smoking only in designated outdoor areas.
  • No wild parties.
  • Please treat the house, the furniture and the equipment with care.
  • Keep valuables in the safe.

Cameras

There are outdoor cameras at the entrance. If anything is not working or you need help, message us on WhatsApp or Zalo.

Frequently asked questions

Can we use the karaoke in the evening?

Yes. Loud music only until 21:30, so plan the singing for the early evening.

Can we smoke on the rooftop?

Smoking is only allowed in designated outdoor areas, never indoors.

What happens if we arrive late?

Thanks to self check-in with the keypad, you can arrive within the check-in window without waiting for anyone.

Questions about the rules? Message us on WhatsApp or Zalo, or check your dates on Airbnb or Trip.com.
