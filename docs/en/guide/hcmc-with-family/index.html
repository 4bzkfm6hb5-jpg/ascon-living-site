Things to do in Ho Chi Minh City with kids

Saigon is busy, warm and very family-friendly if you plan a little.

Practical tips

  • It is warm all year: plan outdoor sightseeing for the morning or late afternoon and rest at midday.
  • Traffic is dense and motorbikes are everywhere – hold children's hands when crossing streets.
  • Ride-hailing apps such as Grab are widely used and make getting around with a family simple.
  • Drink bottled or filtered water.
  • A whole house with its own kitchen makes meals and nap times easier for small children.

Ideas for the day

  • Saigon Zoo and Botanical Gardens (Thảo Cầm Viên), one of the oldest zoos in Vietnam
  • Parks and riverside walks in Phú Mỹ Hưng
  • Ben Thanh Market in the city centre
