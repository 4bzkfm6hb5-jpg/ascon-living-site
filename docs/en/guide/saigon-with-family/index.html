Where to stay in Saigon

If you travel as a family or group, ASCON LIVING is a whole house in Ho Chi Minh City with three en-suite bedrooms, a karaoke room and a rooftop BBQ terrace. Self check-in, airport transfer on request. Check dates for the house →

Check availability

How the house fits a family

  • 3 bedrooms, each with its own bathroom and air conditioning.
  • A kitchen for meals with children, and a washing machine.
  • Bedroom 3 has no window, so it stays dark and quiet for a nap.
  • An entertainment room with karaoke and a 4K cinema system (Optoma 4K projector) for time indoors.

How the house fits a small group

Friends share the living room, the kitchen and the rooftop, and still sleep in separate bedrooms. The rooftop has a BBQ. Loud music is only until 21:30.

Getting around

  • Hold children's hands when crossing streets. Motorbikes are everywhere.
  • Ride-hailing such as Grab is widely used.
  • An airport transfer from Tân Sơn Nhất can be arranged on request with your booking.
  • Self check-in is by keypad. The outer gate uses a key.
