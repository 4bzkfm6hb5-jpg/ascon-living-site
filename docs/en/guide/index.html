The free ASCON Guide App

The heart of the guide is the ASCON Guide App, our own guest guide for the city. It is free, needs no password and opens in your phone's browser, so there is nothing to install from an app store, and you can add it to your home screen.

How it helps you get around

Every place is listed with its distance from the house and estimated travel times, and one tap on Route gives you directions from your front door. The map shows everything around you at a glance, so you can plan a day that makes sense.

What's inside

Food, starting with our Top 10 near the house; cafés and desserts; bars and nightlife; kids and family; sights and culture; activities; relaxation; shopping; day trips; and practical and emergency information, from arrival at Tân Sơn Nhất Airport to show-to-driver cards and an SOS button.

Travelling with kids

Ho Chi Minh City is a great family destination when you plan around the heat and the traffic. Our guide to things to do in Ho Chi Minh City with kids has practical tips and ideas for the day, and the Kids & family category in the app collects outings for children.

Plan B for rainy days

The house itself has an entertainment room with a 4K projector, karaoke and a PlayStation, so a rainy afternoon at home is never wasted.

From the airport to the house

Our airport transfer guide explains your options from Tân Sơn Nhất. The simplest way is the airport transfer, which you can arrange with your booking. With self check-in by keypad and a key for the outer gate, you can arrive at any time.
