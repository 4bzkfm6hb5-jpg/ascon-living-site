Family accommodation District 7: a whole house for your family

Travelling with kids, grandparents or the whole extended family? At ASCON LIVING you have the entire house to yourselves, with a bathroom for every bedroom and plenty of space to play, eat and rest.

Why families feel at home here

Family trips work best when everyone has room to breathe. In a hotel, that usually means two or three separate rooms, often on different floors, and a lot of time spent in corridors and lifts. At ASCON LIVING the whole family stays under one roof, in one private house with over 250 m² of living space across three floors plus a rooftop terrace.

There are three bedrooms, and each one has its own bathroom and its own air conditioning. Parents can take the master bedroom of about 55 m² with its 180×200 cm bed, balcony and 55-inch 4K TV. Bedrooms 2 and 3 each have a 160×200 cm bed and a smart TV, perfect for kids, teenagers or grandparents. Bedroom 3 is quiet and dark, which makes it ideal for afternoon naps and for little ones who wake up with the sun. A guest WC in the living area saves trips upstairs during the day.

No other guests share the house, so nobody minds if the kids are lively in the morning, and you never have to keep them quiet in a hallway. In short: a 3-bedroom house that works as a family-friendly homestay, with all the privacy of your own home.

Meals the way your family likes them

Picky eaters, early breakfasts, baby food, a craving for something familiar after a week of new flavours: a real kitchen solves all of that. The kitchen has a stove, an oven, a microwave, a rice cooker, a kettle and all the dishes you need, plus a large fridge for milk, fruit, snacks and drinks.

Cook a simple pasta for the kids and try something Vietnamese for yourselves, or bring back takeaway from the street food stalls and restaurants nearby and eat together at the dining table. On a warm evening, carry dinner up to the rooftop and grill on the BBQ with the river and the District 1 skyline in view.

Rainy afternoons and family movie nights

Every family holiday needs a plan B, and in Saigon's rainy season you'll be glad to have one. The entertainment room turns an afternoon indoors into a highlight: a movie night with the 4K projector and Netflix, a family karaoke contest, or a PlayStation tournament where the kids finally get to beat their parents. The living room with its L-shaped sofa and 75-inch 4K TV is the second place to gather for cartoons or a family film.

When the kids are asleep, the grown-ups can head up to the rooftop for a quiet drink under the solar lights. Loud music only until 21:30 means everyone gets a good night's sleep.

Safe, simple and stress-free

Parents think about safety first, so here are the facts. Cameras are installed outdoors only, never inside. The house has a safe for passports and valuables, and self check-in with a keypad lock means you can arrive whenever your flight lands, with a separate key for the outer gate.

Travelling with luggage, car seats and tired kids is easier with a transfer, so an airport transfer can be arranged together with your booking. Fresh bedding, towels, toiletries, slippers and hot water are ready when you arrive, an iron is on hand, and a laundry service is available for longer stays.
